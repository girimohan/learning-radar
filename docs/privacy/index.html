Privacy

Learning Radar has no accounts, no ads and no cookies. This notice explains the little that is processed.

Who is responsible

Barmo (Business ID 3111862-6), Vantaa, Finland. Contact: barmo.fin@gmail.com.

Your study plan

Courses you add to "My plan" are saved in your own browser (local storage). They never leave your device and we cannot see them. Clearing your browser data, or "Clear all" in the plan, deletes them.

Visitor statistics

We do not use any analytics or visitor tracking.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, to deliver it and keep the service secure; see GitHub's privacy statement. The site loads no fonts, scripts or images from other services.

When you contact us

If you email us or report an error, we use your message and address only to answer and fix the problem, and delete them when they are no longer needed. Reports on GitHub are public and covered by GitHub's terms.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted, by emailing us. You can also complain to the Data Protection Ombudsman in Finland: tietosuoja.fi.

Learning Radar is an independent service and is not affiliated with the Finnish National Agency for Education, Skolverket or Utdanning.no. Details come from each provider's listing and can change: always check the course page before you apply.
