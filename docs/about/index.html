About Learning Radar

Learning Radar collects courses, training, vocational qualifications and degrees in Finland, Sweden, Norway and online from public sources, three times a week, and ranks them by what they can do for your career. It is free, has no ads and needs no account. It is built and run by Barmo, a small software studio in Vantaa, Finland.

Check before you apply

Learning Radar is an independent service. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the Finnish National Agency for Education (Opetushallitus), Skolverket, Utdanning.no or any course provider. Costs, dates, languages and eligibility come from each provider's own listing and are read automatically, so they can be out of date or wrong. Always check the course page, and for benefits your employment office, Kela, CSN or Lånekassen, before you apply. Nothing here is advice about your own situation.

How the score works

Each offer gets a score from 0 to 100. It is a ranking aid, not a judgement of quality.

  • What you walk away with (28%): a degree or qualification counts most, then a certification, then university credits.
  • What it costs (30%): free and publicly funded first, then free to learn with a paid certificate, then paid.
  • Field (22%): fields where employers are looking for people, such as software, data, cybersecurity, health care, energy and engineering, score higher.
  • Timing (12%): an application deadline in the next three months, or a course you can start any time.
  • Fits around a job (8%): online, self-paced, part-time, evenings or weekends.

Offers first seen in the last 7 days and offers whose deadline is in the next 21 days get a few extra points (4 and 4). Offers under 30 are not shown. The reasons under each course show what counted.

Sources and licences

  • Opintopolku (Studyinfo.fi), the Finnish National Agency for Education's national study portal: degrees, vocational qualifications, continuing education, open university and Jotpa-funded training in Finland. opintopolku.fi
  • Skolverket, Susa-navet, the Swedish national register of education: university courses and programmes, higher vocational education, labour market training and folk high schools. skolverket.se
  • Utdanning.no: higher education programmes in Norway's national admission and vocational college (fagskole) programmes. Contains data under the Norwegian licence for Open Government data (NLOD) made available by Utdanning.no. NLOD 2.0
  • MOOC.fi (University of Helsinki), FutureLearn, Coursera and Microsoft Learn: course titles, descriptions and links from their public catalogues.

Course names, descriptions and trademarks belong to their owners. Every course links to the provider's own page. If you run one of these services or offer a course and want something changed or removed, please email us.

Report an error

Found a wrong price, date or language? Report it on GitHub (needs a free GitHub account) or email us. Each course also has its own report link in the radar.

Accessibility

We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA. The radar works with a keyboard and screen readers, follows your system's light or dark setting, and the lists on this site work without JavaScript. Known limits: course descriptions come from providers and may be in Finnish, Swedish or Norwegian only. If something does not work for you, please tell us and we will fix it.
